# Tab Builder

Build, preview, and export tabulature directly inside the main app.

The builder includes a **Tap Rhythm (Quantized)** panel: start capture, tap/hold your rhythm, then stop to automatically fill the rhythm row and insert muted `x` placeholders on low E so you can immediately audition and replace them with real fretted notes. Quantization supports regular note grids and triplet grids, plus a Humanize slider (default `0%`, which keeps the current exact quantization behavior). Held taps that cross bar boundaries are split into tied rhythm groups automatically.
